# Sign fingerprint of the anomalies (ID-011, first version 2026-11-24)

Any single new-physics mechanism must push each observable in the direction the
anomalies require. This table records those required directions, so a candidate
mechanism can be checked in seconds before anyone fits it.

Directions are relative to Planck-calibrated ΛCDM. "?" means no clear requirement.

| Anomaly | Late expansion H(z < 1) | Sound horizon r_d | Clustering z < 1 | Clustering / lensing z ≈ 1–3 | Isotropy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hubble tension (local H0 high) | higher at z ≈ 0 | or smaller | ? | ? | – |
| Evolving DE (w0 > -1, wa < 0) | DE density falling today, so H(z≈0) lower relative to Λ | ? | ? | ? | – |
| Negative neutrino mass | lower Ωm in BAO geometry | ? | ? | **more** (excess CMB lensing) | – |
| S8 (DES Y6, 2.6σ projected; full-parameter 1.8σ) | ? | ? | **less** (weak) | ? | – |
| Cosmic dipole excess | – | – | – | – | **broken** (or unexplained structure) |
| Birefringence | – | – | – | – | parity broken in the photon sector |

## Conflicts the table exposes

1. **Evolving DE vs Hubble tension.** w0 > -1 means dark energy is fading today, which
   lowers the inferred H0. The literature notes the DESI signal is
   [in conflict with the Hubble tension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04417). A single
   late-time mechanism cannot easily fix both. Only a combination works: an early-time
   change shrinking r_d (for H0) plus late-time DE evolution.
2. **More clustering at z ≈ 1–3, less at z < 1** (if the DES S8 hint is real). This needs
   growth that is boosted earlier and suppressed later. It is a specific shape that
   rules out mechanisms changing growth in one direction only.
3. **Isotropy and parity anomalies** are not fixable by any FLRW background change. They
   are either separate physics or systematics, so they should be screened separately
   from the background-expansion anomalies.

## Cross-epoch link (ID-024)

If the CMB lensing excess is a real ~4% amplitude excess that extends to z ≈ 10, rare
halos hosting JWST's bright galaxies become ×1.6–2.3 more common at z = 10 and
×1.9–3.2 at z = 12. That is the same direction as the JWST excess, which adds a third
entry to the "more structure early" column. It sharpens conflict 2: whatever boosts
early structure must switch off or reverse by z < 1.

## Use

Before proposing or fitting a mechanism, fill in its row. A mechanism must match the
bold entries it claims to explain, and must not reverse any other entry.
