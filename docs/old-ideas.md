# Old ideas revisited against today's anomalies (2026-11-24)

| Old idea (origin) | Modern echo | Today's status | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hoyle–Narlikar C-field** (1960s steady state): negative-energy field that creates matter | It *is* the phantom field ([Sahni primer](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0904.3445)). DESI's early-time phantom phase (w < −1) has this ancestry; matter creation = energy exchange between DE and matter | Interacting-phantom models are active; the C-field framing adds a physical reason for energy exchange | **known, worth keeping in mind**: phantom crossing plus energy transfer is a 60-year-old package |
| **Dirac Large Numbers / varying G** (1937), **Teller's constraint** (1948) | Varying G would shift SN Ia luminosity through the Chandrasekhar mass, and could warm the faint young Sun | Both need \|Ġ/G\| ≈ 2.5–5×10⁻¹² /yr; lunar laser ranging gives (0.2 ± 0.7)×10⁻¹² ([Le+ 2024 summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11227554)). Excluded at 3.3σ and 6.7σ if the rate is constant (ID-029) | **dead** unless G varied in the past and froze recently |
| **Teller's argument itself** | — | It was built on an H0 that was off by ~7× ([Peebles 2016](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.06474)) | **lesson**: constraints inherit the errors of the H0 of their day |
| **Lemaître–Eddington loitering / coasting** (1927–1930s) | More time at high z for JWST galaxies | Revived mainly in fringe models (R_h = ct, CCC); classic loitering conflicts with CMB; JWST explainable in ΛCDM | **parked** |
| **Eckart bulk viscosity** (1940), Murphy (1973) | Viscous pressure mimics phantom DE; could switch on with structure formation | Early-time version disfavoured by DESI DR2 ([Velten & Iania 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05148)); late-time versions active | **known** |
| **Einstein–Straus (1945), Ellis "fitting problem" (1984)** → backreaction / timescape | Apparent acceleration from averaging an inhomogeneous universe | Timescape claims very strong Bayesian preference (ln B > 5) over ΛCDM on Pantheon+, and that DESI's evolving DE matches its 2009 prediction ([Seifert+ 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15143)) | **tested (ID-032, 032b): disfavoured.** SNe alone: ΛCDM better by Δχ² ≈ 2–5. Adding DESI BAO: ΛCDM better by Δχ² ≈ 29 (25 with a 1% BAO systematic), because SNe and BAO demand different void fractions (≈0.8 vs ≈0.69) |
| **Baade's two populations** (1952) | Population-mixing errors in standard candles | See docs/history-lessons.md (ID-031) | **search rule** |
